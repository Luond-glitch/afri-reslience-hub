Who We Are

AfriResilience Hub for Inclusive Climate Action (ARHICA) is a community-based non-profit rooted in Kisumu City within the Lake Victoria Basin.

Empowering Communities for Climate Resilience

We empower youth, women, persons with disabilities, and vulnerable groups to drive inclusive climate resilience and economic transformation.

The Lake Victoria Basin reflects Africa's development crossroads: declining fisheries, climate shocks, youth unemployment, food insecurity, and exclusion from decision-making.

ARHICA responds by turning these challenges into opportunities through community-led, evidence-based, and inclusive solutions that link climate action, food systems, governance, and enterprise development.

The Challenge We Face

The Problem We Are Solving

Climate change and socio-economic challenges threaten livelihoods across Western Kenya's Lake Victoria Basin.

  • Climate change threatening fisheries, agriculture and livelihoods
  • Rising youth unemployment and underemployment
  • Underutilized blue economy opportunities
  • Exclusion from climate decision-making
  • Environmental degradation of Lake Victoria
  • Food insecurity and poverty deepening

Without integrated, inclusive, and scalable interventions, these challenges will continue to deepen poverty, inequality, and vulnerability.

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding our work towards a resilient and inclusive Africa

Our Vision

An Africa where climate resilience and food systems are inclusive, just and community-led.

We envision thriving communities where everyone, especially the most vulnerable, actively shapes their climate-resilient future.

Our Mission

To advance inclusive climate action by strengthening community resilience, transforming food systems, and promoting climate justice through evidence-based, community-led solutions.

We bridge the gap between local innovation and global climate action agendas.

Our Flagship Initiatives

Integrated solutions that demonstrate how climate resilience can drive jobs, inclusion, and economic growth.

SamakiLog Digital Application

Data-driven fisheries and aquaculture value chains. A fully developed digital mobile and computer application designed to strengthen the blue economy across the Lake Victoria Basin.

Key Benefits: Digital record-keeping, improved productivity, market linkages, and youth engagement in tech-driven solutions.

View App

Sustainable Fish Cage Enterprises

Youth employment through scalable aquaculture. Establishing sustainable fish cages along Lake Victoria to create decent employment and enterprise opportunities.

Key Outcomes: Direct employment for youth, skills development in aquaculture, improved household incomes, and local food security.

Get Involved

Environmental Restoration Partnerships

Community-led conservation and climate action. Restoring degraded ecosystems along the Lake Victoria Basin and promoting sustainable natural resource management.

Focus Areas: Ecosystem restoration, community climate action campaigns, and strengthening youth participation in conservation.
