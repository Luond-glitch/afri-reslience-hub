As Founding Visionary and Chairperson of ARHICA's Board of Directors, he provides strategic direction, governance oversight, and policy leadership to advance inclusive, climate-adaptive African communities.

Mr. Cersey Ochieng Onyango

Mr. Cersey Ochieng Onyango, an accomplished Environmentalist and a PhD candidate, is a dedicated researcher bringing over 8 years of extensive experience bridging the gap between academic inquiry and practical field solutions.

Throughout a distinguished career, he has worked with international research and project implementation collaborations with Makerere University, Bowie State University, CoElib-Egerton University, University of Waterloo among others. His professional journey is further defined by 4 years of hands-on expertise in project management and implementation, notably serving as a consultant and field manager for Community Health Support (COHESU-Kenya) and Waterloo University. Mr. Onyango actively participates in a wide array of biodiversity conservation programs, leveraging his comprehensive background in Environmental Science to drive sustainable change and evidence-based decision-making. He is also deeply committed to the intersection of policy and practice and currently serves as a lecturer at Egerton University and as an Associate Editor for Pan Africa Science Journal (PASJ).
